## Publishing routine

Publish one reviewed guide each week, starting at the top unless a live customer question offers a stronger topic. Use a distinct search intent, check facts and current official guidance, add useful links to existing guides, and end with the existing quote form. Keep every guide in one Markdown file. Do not create duplicate city articles by swapping place names.

Before publishing, confirm service claims with John, remove private TODO comments, check the description and related guides, and inspect the page on mobile. Pricing content needs verified MCC figures or an honest explanation of the factors, with no invented averages.
