# Texas partner network city backlog

This is a directional planning order based on the apparent concentration and variety of commercial buildings that could suit this referral network. It is editorial judgment, not a measured building density ranking. Building count, occupied floor area, metropolitan boundaries, and buildings per square mile can produce different orders. Validate commercial property data and partner readiness before committing to a market.

Houston and Dallas Fort Worth are the initial markets and are excluded from these 10 ideas. Target established commercial businesses with at least $1 million in annual revenue, including private property owners and managers. Do not target schools, school districts, government contracts, or residential cleaning on these referral pages. A location in this backlog does not establish partner availability.

## Data and approval needed for every city

Each city data file should contain an accurate market name, surrounding suburbs, supported building types, supported services, coverage notes, and partner status. Record partner identities privately until John approves public use. Vetted status requires an actual review, not merely a completed application.

Before publication, check insurance information, commercial experience, service capability, references where available, communication, geographic coverage, and capacity through a documented human review. Do not invent partners, addresses, testimonials, logos, customer counts, or response times. Explain that MCC serves San Angelo and surrounding West Texas directly and introduces businesses in this market to suitable local partners when available.

Every city page needs its own useful market and service content, a qualified commercial inquiry form, and the disclosure: “Master Commercial Clean may receive a referral fee from partner companies.” Avoid claims of a physical MCC office in a city without one. Review revenue qualification and partner status before making an introduction.
